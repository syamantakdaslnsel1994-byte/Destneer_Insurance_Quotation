## KEY FINDINGS

### Dynamic field behavior (Cover Type: Individual → Floater)
Most plans with Cover Type show **exactly the same fields** for both Individual and Floater.
Plans where Cover Type DOES change the field set:
- **Individual** adds: Age of Member 2 (newMem_2) — appears when Cover Type = Individual AND Total Members >= 2
- **Floater** removes: newMem_2 (age entries for additional members are folded under floater)
- Supreme Enhance additionally shows/hides OPD SI field based on Cover Type

### Plans WITHOUT Cover Type (no dynamic cover)
Secure, POS Secure, Secure Plus, POS Secure Plus, Secure Child, POS Secure Child,
Care Classic, Care Classic-Instant Cover, Super Mediclaim, Cancer Mediclaim,
Student Explore, Student Explore-HU, New Explore, POS Explore, Care Global,
Super Care Advantage, Surrogacy & Ooctye Donor, Care Advantage, Sr Health Advantage

### Add-on fields (do NOT affect premium logic, just configure add-ons)
| Add-on field        | Plans it appears in                         | Options                              |
|---------------------|---------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| PED Tenure          | Supreme, VFM, Super Saver, Ultimate Care    | 1 Year / 2 Year                      |
| NCB Value           | Care Supreme, VFM variants                  | CB Super / CB Booster                |
| Instant Cover       | Care Supreme, VFM variants                  | Instant Cover / Instant Cover Adv    |
| Claim Shield        | Care Supreme, VFM variants                  | Claim Shield Plus                    |
| OPD SI (₹)          | Care, POS Care, Senior, Smart Select        | 5000–50000 (stepped)                 |
| Deductible SI (₹)   | Supreme Enhance, Super Saver, Ultimate Sr   | 25000/50000/100000 or 5L fixed       |

### Plans with unique/unusual fields
- **Super Mediclaim**: Plan Type = Cancer/Critical/Operation/Heart (changes product type entirely)
- **Student Explore**: Policy Tenure in Months (not years), Travel Plan Type
- **New Explore / POS Explore**: Destination zone, trip type, currency, duration in days
- **Care Global**: Global Coverage (WW excl. USA / WW incl. USA), Nationality Status
- **Secure family**: Annual Income + Occupation (Salaried/Business/Other) — income-linked underwriting
- **Surrogacy**: Fixed 3-Year tenure, age 25-35 only, single plan type

### Sum Insured slider (field_2)
Present on all health plans with a range slider (₹5L–₹100L typically).
Backed by hidden input field_2. Portal detects it via the range input's backing hidden field.
